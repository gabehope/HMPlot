# Solar system textures

Surface maps of the Sun and the eight planets, and Saturn's ring texture, from
Solar System Scope, locally resized from the 2K set to 1024×512 (the ring strip
to 1024×62). The planet and Sun maps use equirectangular mapping, longitude −180°
to 180°, with north at the top. The ring texture runs from the inner edge (left)
to the outer edge (right), with the gaps in its alpha channel. Venus shows its
cloud layer.

`stars_milky_way.jpg` is the 2K "stars + Milky Way" map (equirectangular, 2048×1024),
the solar system example's sky effect. It is kept at 2048×1024: as a background all around
the camera it is magnified far more than a planet's map.

Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
