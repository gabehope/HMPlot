# Data

- `quakes-2024.json`: every earthquake of magnitude 4.5 or more in 2024 (6,397 events), from
  the [U.S. Geological Survey's catalog](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/search/), public
  domain: the `lon`, `lat`, `depth` (km) and `mag` columns of `src/examples/earthquakes/quakes-2024.json`.
- The basemap: OpenStreetMap vector tiles from [VersaTiles](https://versatiles.org), loaded while
  the page runs. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) and ESA WorldCover 2021 (CC BY 4.0);
  the plot draws the credit line.
