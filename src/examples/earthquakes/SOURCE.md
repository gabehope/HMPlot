# Data

- `quakes-2024.json`: every earthquake of magnitude 4.5 or more in 2024 (6,397 events), from
  the [U.S. Geological Survey's catalog](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/search/), public
  domain; the 2024 rows of `static/data/earthquakes` (see its `.source.md`), as columns: `month`
  (1 at the start of the year, 13 at its end), `lat`, `lon`, `depth` (km) and `mag`.
- `countries.json`: Natural Earth 1:110m countries, public domain (`static/data/geo`).
