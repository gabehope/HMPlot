# Data

- `color.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation with topography, January 2004, resized to
  4096×2048 (equirectangular, longitude −180° to 180°, north at the top). Credit: NASA Earth
  Observatory / Reto Stöckli.
  Source: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/january/world.topo.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg
- `elevation.png`: the GEBCO elevation display product (grayscale 0–255 for 0–6,400 m; oceans at
  sea level, no underwater relief), resized to 512×256 with Lanczos filtering from the
  4096×2048 copy in `static/examples/earth/elevation.jpg`: the globe's surface is a mesh of
  256 cells a side, so a finer grid adds nothing to its relief. Credit: Jesse Allen, NASA Earth
  Observatory, GEBCO / British Oceanographic Data Centre.
  Source: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/topography/gebco_08_rev_elev_5400x2700.jpg

See https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/topography-bathymetry-maps/.
The color image already includes topographic shading.
