# Data

- `penguins.csv`: the Palmer penguins, 344 penguins of three species (Adelie, Chinstrap, Gentoo)
  measured on three islands of the Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica, 2007 to 2009, by Dr. Kristen
  Gorman and the Palmer Station Long Term Ecological Research Program, from the
  [palmerpenguins](https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/) R package. CC0 1.0 (public
  domain). A copy of `static/data/penguins/penguins.csv`; its `.source.md` has the full source
  note and citation.
