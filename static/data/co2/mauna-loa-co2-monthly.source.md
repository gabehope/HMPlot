# Atmospheric CO2 at Mauna Loa, monthly

Source: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, `co2_mm_mlo.csv`
(https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_mm_mlo.csv, file created 2026-09-05, accessed
2026-09-28). Its notice: "These data are made freely available to the public and the scientific
community... To ensure that GML receives fair credit for their work please include relevant
citation text in publications." Data from March 1958 through April 1974 were obtained by C. David
Keeling of the Scripps Institution of Oceanography. Credit: Dr. Xin Lan, NOAA/GML
(gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/) and Dr. Ralph Keeling, Scripps Institution of Oceanography
(scrippsco2.ucsd.edu/).

`mauna-loa-co2-monthly.csv` holds 822 months, March 1958 to August 2026: `date` (the month's
first day), `co2` (the monthly mean mole fraction, ppm; missing months interpolated by NOAA) and
`deseasonalized` (the same with the average seasonal cycle removed). From December 2022 to July
2023 the measurements come from Maunakea, after the Mauna Loa eruption.
