# Sea-surface temperature for the NetCDF examples

Source: NOAA Extended Reconstructed Sea Surface Temperature, version 5 (ERSST v5), monthly
means on a 2° grid, as `ersstv5.nc` in xarray's tutorial data
(https://github.com/pydata/xarray-data, accessed 2026-09-28). The file's license attribute
reads "No constraints on data access or use"; NOAA asks that ERSST v5 be cited as
Huang et al. (2017), doi:10.1175/JCLI-D-16-0836.1.

`ersst-v5-2014-2016.nc` holds 36 months, January 2014 to December 2016 (the 2015-16 El Niño),
89 latitudes (88 to -88, descending) and 180 longitudes (0 to 358): the variable `sst` (degC;
land is missing), packed as int16 with a scale of 0.01 and compressed, 458 KB. Written with
xarray from the tutorial file:

    ds = xr.open_dataset("ersstv5.nc")
    sub = ds[["sst"]].sel(time=slice("2014-01", "2016-12"))
    sub.to_netcdf("ersst-v5-2014-2016.nc", format="NETCDF4", encoding={
        "sst": {"dtype": "int16", "scale_factor": 0.01, "add_offset": 0.0, "_FillValue": -32768,
                "zlib": True, "complevel": 9, "shuffle": True},
        "time": {"units": "days since 1800-01-01", "calendar": "standard", "dtype": "float64"}})

keeping the `sst`, `lat` and `lon` long names and units and a few global attributes.
